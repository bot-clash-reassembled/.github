## Overview

Bot Clash is a "lifelong project" - I tried to implement the idea of constructing & programming robots to compete with each other several times already:
- **2010**: first Bot Clash title, end-to-end single player game with elements of construction & programming, some story and Laser Squad style visuals - too much to chew, low quality, so got nowhere
- **2011**: Bot Clash Online was a try to refocus initial Bot Clash on multiplayer - total wreck again
- **2013**: Code Drones was browser-based programming in JS against "drone SDK", so your drone can collect golden triangles across the game field and don't bump into obstacles too much
- **2019**: Drone Clash didn't get past the ideation phase but most of its "spirit" made into the BCR

**Bot Clash: Reassembled** aka **BCR** started in 2025 is the culmination of the series:
- Ruthless cutting of anything beyond a simple yet playable core
- Focus on construction & multiplayer but endless possibilities for expansion in other pillars (see below)
- No commercial ambition this time, the whole thing is going to be OSS

Overall Bot Clash as I see it now stands on 4 pillars:
- **Bot construction**: engineering challenge of balancing different parts into cohesive & perfomant design 
- **Bot programming**: programming challenge of building the best possible algorithm of bot's behavior in combat
- **Competition visuals**: up to AAA quality of bot battles appearance 
- **Multiplayer**: sophisticated global gamification of competition between bot creators

## Ideas for next steps

I won't call the following "a roadmap" but rather a bag of ideas on futher development of BCR in area of each pillar:
- **Bot construction**:
  - Multiple hull types
  - Multi-cell part shapes aka "design tetris"
  - Much more different parts organized in a technology tree
  - Various effects of different parts similarly to a sophisticated card game
- **Bot programming**:
  - Customization of bot's behavior vs current hardcoded algo
  - Procedurally generated environments for bot battles beyond empty space as opportunity to leverage the environment in own algos
  - Timing parts' effects for own benefit
- **Competition visuals**:
  - Actual battlefield visualization instead of current imitation
  - Incremental visuals upgrade towards sophisticated modern graphics
- **Multiplayer**:
  - Participation in multiple leagues simultaneously with different designs
  - Overall player rank beyond individual leagues
  - Leagues tiers perhaps linked to part / technology tiers available to participants of each tier
